How the score works

MAI = mean over events of (P × Q × R)

  • P: the manufacturer's share of the event's apex lots. Apex-unknown lots are left out of both counts and shown beside the score.
  • Q: price ÷ high estimate, averaged over apex lots sold with a published price. Uses the house's own currency when the price and estimate are in the same currency, otherwise the recorded USD figures. Lots ranked by sold price have no estimate to compare with, so they are left out of Q.
  • R: sold ÷ (sold + not sold). A sale with the price withheld counts as sold. Withdrawn lots are left out.
  • If nothing sold, R is 0 and so is that event's term. If all of a make's apex lots at a sale were ranked by sold price (Mecum, Barrett-Jackson and duPont REGISTRY Live publish no estimates), its term there is P × R, marked without Q. Such a term reads higher than a full P × Q × R, because Q is usually under 1 and only sold lots can be ranked by price. If something sold but no price is usable, the event is unscored: it is left out, not counted as 0.
  • Events count equally. Makes are grouped by manufacturer (for example, Mercedes-AMG and Maybach under Mercedes-Benz).
  • Scores rest on few lots. Check the apex and apex-unknown columns before reading much into a difference.
