How MII is built

A weighted blend of sale price (20%), bid activity (20%), views (15%), Google Trends (15%), comments (10%), YouTube views (10%), social engagement (5%) and vehicle age (5%). Inputs a row has no value for are dropped and the remaining weights renormalized, never imputed.

Confidence

Based on auctions in the month: High 15+, Medium-High 8+, Medium 4+, Low below 4. Changes smaller than the normal swing for a sample that size are shown in grey with a dotted underline.

Data

Bring a Trailer auction results, refreshed twice daily at 6 AM and 6 PM UTC. Cars & Bids is not included.
