      How MII is built

      A weighted blend of sale price (20%), bid activity (20%), views (15%), Google Trends (15%), comments (10%), YouTube views on leading enthusiast channels (10%), social engagement (5%) and vehicle age (5%), each as a percentile among that month's cars. Inputs a row has no value for are dropped and the remaining weights renormalized, never imputed. Watch the 2-minute explainer.

      Confidence and noise

      Confidence comes from auctions in the month: High 15+, Medium-High 8+, Medium 4+, Low below 4. Changes smaller than the normal swing for a sample that size are shown in grey with a dotted underline, and are never reported as insights.

      Data

      Bring a Trailer auction results, refreshed twice daily. Cars & Bids is not included. BMW CI constituents follow the HAGI index committee list, with the E46 M3 added in 2026.
