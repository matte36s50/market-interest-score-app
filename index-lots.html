Price basis

  • Bring a Trailer: the hammer price, without BaT's buyer's fee (5%, capped at $7,500). At the top of the market that is about 1% under an all-in price; on a $30K car, about 5%.
  • Live auctions: the price the auction store holds. That is the fee-inclusive price where one was entered, otherwise the hammer, and the store doesn't record which, so a live price may or may not include the buyer's premium.
  • Medians mix the two, so a car that sells mostly on Bring a Trailer reads a little low against one that sells mostly at the live houses.
  • Other currencies are in US dollars at the ECB rate: a live sale's date, a completed Bring a Trailer month's last day, or the day a listing closed this month. Estimates of sales still to come use the latest rate.

How cars are matched

  • Each lot is matched to an index car by its title (the Bring a Trailer listing's address, or the sale's year, make and model) against the model years and names in data/variants.csv.
  • Replicas, recreations, tributes, continuation cars and later bodies "in the style of" the original are left out.
  • A sold price under 40% of the car's other results is set aside as most likely not the car the title says (a project, a shell, a mislabelled listing). It stays listed, faded, but counts in no figure.
  • this month Bring a Trailer results from the month in progress. MAI counts a Bring a Trailer month only once it is complete, so these are in no score yet.
  • highlight among its sale's top 10% of lots, the ones the Apex Index scores.
  • race car the title says it is a competition car (Group A, a works rally car, a converted racer). It counts as the car it is, in every figure.
  • Where one of HAGI's cars covers models that sell at different levels, each model has its own row (HAGI's "635CSi & M635CSi" is a 635CSi row and an M635CSi row), so a median is one model's.
  • Cars & Bids is not included. A live lot links to the auction house's own page where the store has one.
